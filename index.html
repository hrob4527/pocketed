Fees are not a rounding error.

Etsy charges a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee on the item and the shipping you charge, and a U.S. payment-processing fee of 3% + $0.25 on the total including sales tax. Offsite Ads can add 15% or 12%, capped at $100 per order. Those are Etsy’s own figures, not ours.

Stripe’s standard U.S. domestic card rate is 2.9% + $0.30. Gumroad direct sales take 10% + $0.50 plus card processing of 2.9% + $0.30 (Discover marketplace sales are a flat 30%, including processing).

Sellers already build spreadsheets for this because payout dashboards show gross more clearly than take-home. Pocketed is that spreadsheet, without the setup, and without sending financial files to somebody else’s server.

Who it’s for

  • Etsy sellers who want the real margin on a listing before they publish it — including Offsite Ads and a tax set-aside.
  • Independent sellers and freelancers who get Stripe or Gumroad payout CSVs and want a monthly “what I kept” number.
  • Anyone who has been surprised by a payout that was much smaller than the sale price.

A related one-page tool, Kept, is already sold on Gumroad as a $12 HTML file that reads a payout CSV and holds back a tax reserve. Pocketed adds official 2026 fee schedules, a listing-price calculator, a reverse “price to hit a target take-home” solver, and printable exports.

Questions

Do you upload my payout files?

No. Parsing happens in this browser. You can disconnect from the network after the page loads and the tool still works, including the sample files packaged with the app.

Is this tax, legal, or accounting advice?

No. The tax reserve is a percentage you choose so you can set money aside. Self-employed people in the United States often owe income tax and self-employment tax and may need to make quarterly estimated payments — confirm filings with a CPA or the IRS. Pocketed does not file anything and does not connect to your shop.

Which fee rates do you use?

Published rates as of 11 November 2026, cited on the Sources section and inside the app. Platforms change fees. If a rate looks stale, check the official page and use Custom fees, or ask for an update.

What if my CSV looks different?

If the usual headers are present (Amount, Fees, Net, Date, Type), mapping is automatic. If not, you can assign columns yourself. Bank-deposit rows are kept out of the sales total so a transfer isn’t counted as a loss.
