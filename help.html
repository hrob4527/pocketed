    Ordering

    How does checkout work?

    Pocketed is a storefront that sends you to two trusted checkouts. Digital tools are sold through Lemon Squeezy, which takes payment, adds any sales tax and emails your download. Merch is sold through Pocketed’s Printify store at pocketed.printify.me, which takes payment, prints your item and ships it. This site never sees your card details.

    Why can’t I pay for everything in one cart?

    Digital files and printed merch are handled by different companies. Your Pocketed bag keeps everything together, then gives you one button per item: “Check out” for digital tools, and “Buy on Printify” for merch. On Printify you can add several merch items to one cart and pay shipping once.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Your bag, saved list and recently viewed items are stored in this browser only. Clearing your browser data clears them.

    Which payment methods are accepted?

    The checkout pages show the options they support, usually major cards and wallets such as Apple Pay or PayPal depending on your device and region.

    Digital downloads

    When do I get my file?

    Right after payment, Lemon Squeezy emails you a download link. Check spam if it isn’t there within a few minutes, or write to us.

    What do I need to open the files?

    Spreadsheets work in Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers. PDFs open in any PDF reader. Templates are Word (DOCX) files that also open in Google Docs and Pages.

    Is this tax or legal advice?

    No. Our calculators and templates help you organize numbers. They don’t replace an accountant or tax professional.

    Merch

    Who makes and ships the merch?

    Printify and its print partners. Each item is printed after you order it, then shipped to you.

    How do sizes run?

    Tees are unisex, sizes S to 2XL. Check the size chart on the Printify product page before ordering, because made-to-order items can’t be exchanged for size.

    Do the tees have tags?

    Our tee blanks (Bella+Canvas 3001 and Gildan 64000) use tear-away neck labels, so you can remove the label if it bothers you.
